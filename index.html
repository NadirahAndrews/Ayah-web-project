آية

01 / A VERSE

Ayah

/ aa-yah /

An ayah is a verse of the Quran. the word also means "sign". A single ayah is a small starting point for a bigger exploration.

سورة

02 / A CHAPTER

Surah

/ soo-rah /

A surah is a chapter of the Quran. There are 114 surahs, each made up of ayahs. Reading the surrounding verses helps you see and understand the passage more fully.

معنى

03 / A WAY TO UNDERSTAND

Translation

/ meaning in another language

An English translation helps you approach the meaning of the Arabic text. It is a translator's rendering, and does not replace the original or a detailed commentary.
